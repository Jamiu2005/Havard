The Torchbearer of SUDAI's Vision

In 2015, after the passing of the visionary leader Late Alhaji Sanni Yusuf, Alhaji Mustapha Olaide Carew took up the role of Chief Missioner of SUDAI. His appointment as Chief Missioner & Chief Missionary of 3-Man-Committee set the stage for a new era of growth and development.

In 2010, after the passing of the visionary leader Late Alhaji Sheikh (Dr.) Abd. Hameed Aweda Olohungbemi (R.T.A), a.k.a. Ayeogbaro, Faddelat Sheikh Alhaji (Dr.) Abd. Waheed Badrudeen, a.k.a. Temitope, ascended as the Khalifah and Chief Executive & Chief Missionary of Shams-Ud-Deen Al-Islamiy (SUDAI) worldwide.

His leadership marked a new era for SUDAI, one where the foundational principles set by the late Sheikh Aweda would continue to evolve and expand, reaching new heights both spiritually and organizationally.

A New Chapter for SUDAI

Khalifah Abd. Waheed’s journey to leadership was a significant transition for SUDAI. His ability to uphold the values and vision of Sheikh Abd. Hameed (R.T.A) while driving the organization forward was central to his leadership. Under his stewardship, SUDAI expanded its reach and influence beyond Nigeria, touching lives globally while staying true to the principles of moral, spiritual, and educational reformation. While the loss of Sheikh Abd. Hameed (R.T.A) was profound, Khalifah Abd. Waheed approached his new responsibilities with unwavering faith and a sense of duty. He sought to ensure that the foundation of SUDAI remained intact, and at the same time, pushed the organization to adapt to the challenges of a rapidly changing world.

Global Expansion

Under Khalifah Abd. Waheed’s leadership, SUDAI experienced significant global expansion. While SUDAI initially grew within Nigeria, Khalifah Abd. Waheed envisioned a movement that would touch hearts across borders. With his strategic direction, SUDAI opened new divisions in Africa, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, bringing the organization’s message of faith, unity, and empowerment to communities around the world. Each international expansion marked a new milestone for SUDAI, as Khalifah Abd. Waheed worked tirelessly to establish and strengthen SUDAI’s presence globally. His efforts in building cross-cultural bridges and promoting interfaith dialogue ensured that SUDAI's values resonated with people from all walks of life.

The Legacy of Sheikh Abd. Hameed (R.T.A)

Although Khalifah Abd. Waheed introduced innovative changes, he remained deeply committed to preserving the legacy of Sheikh Abd. Hameed (R.T.A). Every decision and every new initiative was driven by the vision set by the founder, ensuring that SUDAI stayed on course. Khalifah Abd. Waheed’s leadership has been one of continuity — maintaining the organization’s core values while also adapting and evolving to meet contemporary challenges.

Impact on SUDAI Today

Today, under Khalifah Abd. Waheed’s leadership, SUDAI continues to thrive and grow. The core programs — focusing on youth development, education, community service, and spiritual growth — remain central to SUDAI’s mission. At the same time, Khalifah Abd. Waheed has introduced new programs and initiatives that align with the evolving needs of the Muslim Ummah, ensuring that SUDAI’s impact continues to expand.

Looking Forward: Continuing the Vision

As SUDAI continues to grow, Khalifah Abd. Waheed remains focused on carrying forward the legacy of Sheikh Abd. Hameed (R.T.A), ensuring that the organization’s mission of moral and spiritual reformation continues to guide future generations. Khalifah Abd. Waheed’s leadership reflects both the history and future of SUDAI, making it a beacon of hope, education, and unity for Muslims worldwide.
