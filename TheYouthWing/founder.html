FOUDER'S LEGACY

A Vision That Lives On

The legacy of Late Alhaji Sheikh (Dr.) Abd. Hameed Aweda Olohungbemi (R.T.A),
a.k.a. Ayeogbaro, lives on through every branch, zone, and individual empowered by
his vision.

Empowering the Next Generation

One of Sheikh Aweda’s most enduring contributions is the emphasis he placed on empowering youth. He recognized that the future of the Muslim Ummah lay in the hands of the younger generation, and he dedicated much of his efforts to providing them with the tools to succeed spiritually, intellectually, and socially. Under his leadership, SUDAI launched several youth programs, educational initiatives, and community outreach efforts aimed at shaping the next generation of Muslim leaders.

His vision for the moral, educational, and spiritual reformation of the Muslim Ummah continues to
inspire and guide the organization today. His leadership, wisdom, and unshakable faith in Allah
(SWT) laid the foundation for a global movement that reaches Muslims worldwide, fostering a sense
of unity, purpose, and unwavering commitment to the teachings of Islam.

A Visionary Leader with a Higher Purpose

Sheikh Aweda Olohungbemi was not just a leader; he was a visionary who understood the deep needs
of the Muslim community. He saw a world where Muslims were not only spiritually enlightened but
also well-educated, morally strong, and socially responsible. In 1982, he founded SUDAI with the
singular goal of uniting Muslims in pursuit of these values. His belief in the transformative power
of education, spirituality, and social action shaped the programs and initiatives that SUDAI continues to
offer today

He recognized the importance of nurturing the younger generation, empowering them to become leaders who would not only carry the message of Islam but also work for the betterment of society. This forward-thinking approach allowed SUDAI to make an enduring impact on its members and the broader community.

His belief in the importance of leadership training is evident in the SUDAI Youth Wing, which remains a cornerstone of the organization. Today, thousands of young Muslims continue to benefit from the educational and leadership programs Sheikh Aweda set in motion, a testament to his foresight and commitment to the future.

A Legacy of Service to Humanity

Sheikh Aweda’s legacy was also deeply rooted in service to humanity. He believed that Islam was not just a personal faith but a guide for how one should interact with the world. He dedicated his life to uplifting those in need, irrespective of their background or faith. His teachings encouraged SUDAI members to serve their communities, promote justice, and provide assistance to the less privileged.

This commitment to community service continues to be a driving force behind SUDAI’s outreach programs, which focus on social welfare, charity, and supporting the most vulnerable in society. His legacy lives on in every act of kindness and every initiative aimed at making a positive difference in the world.

A Lasting Impact on the Muslim Ummah

While Sheikh Aweda’s passing in 2010 marked the end of an era, his influence continues to shape SUDAI. His vision remains the guiding force behind the organization, and his teachings continue to inspire every member, leader, and follower of SUDAI.

The values he instilled — of unity, education, social responsibility, and spiritual growth — are woven into the fabric of SUDAI’s programs. His legacy is not just in the words he spoke or the actions he took but in the lives he touched and the movement he ignited. Today, as SUDAI expands across the globe, his impact is felt in every country, in every community, and in every individual striving to live a life aligned with the principles of Islam.

Continuing His Vision: SUDAI’s Path Forward

As we look toward the future, SUDAI remains dedicated to the vision set forth by Sheikh Aweda. Under the continued leadership of Faddelat Sheikh Alhaji (Dr.) Abd. Waheed Badrudeen a.k.a. Temitope, SUDAI is not just preserving his legacy but also evolving it to meet the needs of the modern world. The mission remains clear: to promote the moral, spiritual, and educational reformation of Muslims worldwide.

Sheikh Aweda’s vision continues to guide us as we expand our outreach, empower the youth, and engage in global efforts for positive change. Every step we take in building a better future is a step in honor of the legacy of Sheikh Aweda Olohungbemi.
