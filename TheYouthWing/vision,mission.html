ABOUT US

A Movement for a Purpose-Driven Generation

The SUDAI Youth Wing is the heart of Shams-Ud-Deen Al-Islamiy's mission to empower the next generation of Muslim leaders.

Established to foster spiritual growth, educational development, and community engagement, we aim to cultivate a generation of disciplined, compassionate, and knowledgeable youth who are committed to the values of Islam.

Our Youth Wing is open to all Muslims aged 1 day to 45 years, whether married or not, and we are united by a shared commitment to Islamic teachings, the Qur'an, and the Sunnah. Through our programs, leadership development, and community outreach, we strive to create a better future for the Ummah and the world at large.

VOICE OF THE YOUTH

Inspiring Stories from Our Youth

Hear directly from our youth members about their journeys, experiences, and growth.

Being part of the SUDAI Youth Wing has transformed my life. I've developed leadership skills, deepened my understanding of Islam, and found a supportive community that feels like family.

Ahmed Ibrahim

Youth Leader, 22 years old

The Quranic studies program changed my relationship with the Quran. I went from barely being able to read to memorizing several surahs and understanding their meanings. The mentorship I received was invaluable.

Fatima Hassan

Quran Program Graduate, 19 years old

Through the community service initiatives, I've learned that my faith is not just about personal spirituality but about making a positive impact in the world. The monthly service days have become the highlight of my month.

Yusuf Omar

Community Service Volunteer, 25 years old
